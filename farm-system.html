FARM TIME MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Project description: On-premises HR & Payroll System for an agricultural business, replacing a paper-based rostering and wage calculation system with a digital solution covering clock in/out, timesheet management, payroll processing, and compliance reporting.

Project type: Academic Project — Flinders University, Master of Information Technology

Project period: Jun 2025 - Dec 2025

Position: Business Analyst / Project Manager

Product: On-premises HR & Payroll System

Main Features

Employee clock in/out and digital time tracking

Roster and timesheet management

Payroll processing and wage calculation

Compliance reporting

My Responsibilities

Led project planning, requirements analysis, and technical coordination, documenting functional and non-functional requirements, user stories, and As-Is/To-Be business processes.

Maintained risk, issue, and decision logs to support Agile delivery planning and proactive risk management.

Designed improved workflows for the proposed solution, targeting a 60% reduction in manual payroll handling steps and elimination of paper-based time tracking.
